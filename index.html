General settings
Mount calibration

Mount the phone, keep the boat still and level, then tap Set zero. You can also long-press the heel indicator in Racing Mode.

Damping

One level for all values (heel, SOG, heading). Higher = steadier display, slower response.

Do Not Disturb

Automatic Do Not Disturb comes with the native app. For now, turn on Do Not Disturb manually from the phone's quick settings.

Racing mode

Drag ≡ to change the swipe order. The switch shows or hides a screen. Numbers show the order in Racing mode.

Camera

The camera button in Racing mode records video in sync with the telemetry, so in Debrief the video plays with your speed and heading at the same moment. It turns off by itself when tracking stops. Keep the app on screen while recording.
